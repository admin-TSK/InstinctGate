# InstinctGate

## Hero

Your coding agent should get smarter after every session.

InstinctGate captures instincts from the run, promotes the winners into skills, and scores verification so you know the session actually stuck.

## Subhead

A thin bolt-on for Cursor and Claude. Paid cloud product with a 7-day trial. Account and card required. Capture, promote, score, with hosted vault sync and a web scoreboard.

## Problem

Agents forget. You rediscover the same pitfall next week. Giant skill packs look impressive and still do not learn from *your* last hour of work.

## Product

1. **Capture** - turn a short session summary into 1 to 5 instinct candidates with confidence.
2. **Promote** - graduate a strong instinct into a SKILL.md stub you can reuse.
3. **Score** - plan, tests, review, verify. A simple scoreboard, not vibes.

Compatible with Cursor rules and Claude skills. Hosted sync, web scoreboard, and multi-device recall are part of the paid product.
